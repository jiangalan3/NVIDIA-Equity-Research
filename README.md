# NVIDIA Equity Research & Financial Analysis

**FY2024–FY2025 | Microsoft Excel**

## Overview

This project analyzes NVIDIA's financial performance from FY2024 to FY2025, with a focus on revenue growth, profitability, operating performance, and market platform performance.

The goal of the project was to develop practical experience in financial statement analysis and equity research while evaluating the key drivers behind NVIDIA's recent growth.

## Key Findings

- Revenue increased from approximately $60.9B in FY2024 to $130.5B in FY2025.
- Net income increased from approximately $29.8B to $72.9B.
- Data Center revenue was the company's primary growth driver.
- Gross margin increased from approximately 72.7% to 75.0%.
- NVIDIA experienced significant operating leverage as operating income grew faster than revenue.
